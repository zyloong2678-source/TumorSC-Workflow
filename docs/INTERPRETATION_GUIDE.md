# Interpretation guide

- **Cell annotation:** marker expression is context dependent. Review cluster
  quality, doublets, tissue biology, and multiple concordant markers.
- **Candidate malignant cells:** CNV-like signals and tumor-associated markers
  are complementary evidence, not a definitive ground truth.
- **Differential expression:** effect sizes, detection fractions, donor/sample
  structure, and multiple testing matter. Cell-level tests do not replace
  patient-level replication.
- **Pathways:** enrichment depends on the ranked statistic and gene-set source;
  a pathway name is not proof of pathway activation.
- **Communication:** ligand-receptor co-expression predicts communication
  potential and remains hypothesis-generating.
- **Pseudotime:** inferred ordering does not demonstrate real-time progression
  or causal transitions.
- **TF activity:** regulon-based scores infer transcriptional consistency with
  a regulator; they do not directly measure biochemical activity.
